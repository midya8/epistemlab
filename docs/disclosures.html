Every Boolean formula over the supplied atoms is constant within a valuation class. A restriction that splits a class is only a diagnostic what-if, not a Boolean announcement. This is not a claim that it is expressible by an epistemic formula either.

Goals are evaluated after restriction, not before. Inclusion-maximal success means no successful Boolean disclosure retains a strict superset. Maximum-retained success keeps the largest number of supplied worlds. Neither measures formula size or real information leakage.
